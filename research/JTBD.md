# JTBD — Jobs to be done

Source: JTBD.pdf (1 page)

## Functional JTBD
When I'm occupied with work and not physically present in the kitchen, I want to be automatically informed before essential grocery items run out, so I can reorder in time without disrupting my cook's routine or my mealtime.

## Emotional JTBD
When I repeatedly find out at the last moment that something has run out, I want to feel in control of my household without having to actively monitor it, so I don't carry the background anxiety of "what's going to run out next" on top of an already busy day.

## Social JTBD
When I am the person my cook and flatmates depend on to manage grocery reordering, I want to stay on top of it reliably, so I'm seen as a responsible and organised person running the household — not someone who always drops the ball on basic things.
