User Interviews Questions
Warm up (2-3 min)

Tell me about yourself
Tell me a bit about your daily routine  do you cook yourself or do you have help at home?
Who mostly handles the kitchen  you, your partner, or the cook/helper?

Core (15-20 min)

3. Walk me through the last time you ran out of something at home  what happened, how did you find out?

4. Who told you  and when exactly did they tell you? Like, same day you needed it, or before?

5. What did you do immediately after finding out?

6. How did that make you feel in that moment  was it a big deal or just a minor inconvenience?

7. How often does this happen in a month, roughly?

8. Have you ever tried any system to track what's running low at home  a list, an app, anything? How did that go?
Lead time specific (5-7 min)

9. If an app had warned you 3 days before that item was going to run out  would you have acted on it, or ignored it?
