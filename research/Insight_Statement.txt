Insight Statement
Urban working professionals who rely on a cook to manage their kitchen 
struggle to act on early stockout signals 
 even when they're given enough warning 
 because individual low-priority items don't feel worth 
the effort of ordering alone, and passive tracking systems (sticky notes, 
cart-as-wishlist) get abandoned the moment something more urgent 
competes for attention, which means the design must convert 
awareness into action automatically by bundling low-priority items 
together and removing the moment-of-decision effort, rather than simply 
notifying people earlier or asking them to track manually.
