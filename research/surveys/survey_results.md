# Survey / Quantitative — Google Forms chart screenshots

Source: Molecule assignments.zip (image 10–14). Each chart labeled 15 responses.

## Q1 — Stockout frequency (image 10.png)
**How often do you run out of a grocery item at home without realizing it in advance?**
n=15 · pie

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| 2 to 3 times a month | 46.7% |
| Once a week or more | 33.3% |
| Rarely or almost never | 13.3% |
| Once a month | ~6.7% (small slice, unlabeled on chart) |

Combined: ~80% run out unexpectedly at least ~2×/month or more.

## Q3 — Cart / minimum order friction (image 12.png)
**Has a low order value ever (to save delivery/extra charge) stopped you from reordering a grocery item you needed?**
n=15 · pie

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Yes often I wait until I have enough to order | 60% |
| Yes sometimes | 33.3% |
| No I just order whatever I need | ~6.7% |
| I was not aware there was a minimum order value | 0% |

Combined: ~93.3% have been blocked by low order value at least sometimes.

## Q4 — Lead time by category (image 13.png)
**How many days in advance would you want to be notified before each item runs out?**
n=15 · clustered bars · approximate counts from chart

| Preference | Vegetables & fruits | Staples (dal rice atta) | Masalas & spices |
|---|---|---|---|
| Same day | ~4 | 0 | 0 |
| 1 to 2 days | ~9 | ~7 | ~7 |
| 3 to 7 days | ~2 | ~5 | ~6 |
| 1 week or more | 0 | ~2 | ~1 |

Pattern: 1–2 days dominates all categories; same-day only appears for veg/fruit; longer windows more common for staples/masalas.

## Q5 — Bundled notification + ₹150 threshold (image 14.png)
**If an app notified you that multiple grocery items were running low and your combined reorder value crossed 150 INR how likely are you to act on it immediately?**
n=15 · pie

| Answer | % |
|---|---|
| Likely | 53.3% |
| Very likely | 26.7% |
| Unlikely I do not trust the apps prediction | 13.3% |
| Unlikely I would probably defer it | ~6.7% |

Combined: ~80% likely or very likely to act when bundled past ₹150.
