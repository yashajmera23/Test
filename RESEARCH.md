## Research question
How much lead time do users need before a predicted grocery shortage for an early alert to feel useful rather than premature or too late?

## Methods
- **Primary:** User interviews (reflect on timing/urgency)
- **Secondary:** Survey (cross-check prevalence); app-store reviews planned but **not collected**

## Sample
- **Interviews:** P1 Ritika (self-managing, Bangalore); P2 (cook-dependent, Mumbai PG) — n=2
- **Survey:** n=15 — 12 cook/household-help dependent (inferred from recruitment filter), 3 self-managing Blinkit users added later

## Key findings (see `research/affinity_and_insights.md` for full)
1. Two user types — P2 is the target segment (structural, frequent pain)
2. Cook is the information system — problem is forget-to-act, not lack of warning
3. Cart value threshold (₹150) blocks single low-value reorders
4. Lead time is category-dependent (veg same-day, staples 3–7 days, masalas weekly+)
5. Workaround intensity = pain intensity
6. Notification fatigue — must bundle and persist to be actionable
