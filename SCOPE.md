# SCOPE — Blinkit Home Pantry Tracker

> **Status:** Draft from original scope card. Mechanism drift flagged in LOG.md — rewrite hypothesis before build.

## Product
Quick Commerce — Blinkit

## Feature
A virtual shelf that auto-populates when a user orders groceries to their home address. Tracks estimated stock from (a) Blinkit order history and (b) category-level consumption benchmarks. On first setup asks household size to calibrate consumption. Reminds before predicted runout with one-tap reorder.

## Target user
23–35 year old working professionals earning ~₹1L/month in metro cities, living with household help (cook/helper) who manages the kitchen. They don't actively track groceries — find out something is over only when the cook tells them.

## AARRR stage
- **Primary:** Retention
- **Secondary:** Revenue

## Metrics
- **Primary:** D14 retention rate / weekly active sessions
- **Secondary:** Avg orders per user per month; reorder conversion from reminders

## Hypothesis (original — needs update)
Automatically tracking home grocery stock for working professionals who rely on household help will reduce unplanned stockouts and create a habit loop around Blinkit — measured by D14 retention and monthly order frequency.
